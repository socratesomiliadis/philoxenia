Quick story time.

We were 19 when we decided we wanted to move to San Francisco. We didn’t have a network, much money, or any idea where to begin. We just couldn’t stop thinking about it.

Three years, a lot of unanswered emails, and a few people saying yes later, our team is here. But it shouldn’t take all that just to get in the room.

So, this is us opening the door.

Dinos & Takis

Who is Philoxenia for?

Builders aged 15–25, based anywhere in Europe. You should be working on projects and curious about building a company.

How long is the program?

Two weeks in San Francisco and Silicon Valley, spending time with founders, teams and other young builders.

Who is behind the program?

Dinos and Takis, the team behind Hustle Hours. Philoxenia grew out of their own move from Europe to Silicon Valley.
