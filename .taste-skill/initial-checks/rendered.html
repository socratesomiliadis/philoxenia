Two weeks inside the builder’s world.

  1. Build beside founders

    Spend your days with YC founders. Work alongside them and see how a startup gets built, decision by decision.

  2. Visit the teams behind the technology

    Visit the headquarters of companies such as Apple, Google, Meta and OpenAI. Get closer to the work that brought you here.

  3. Meet the campus communities

    See Stanford and Berkeley with local students. Meet people your age who are asking the same questions and building their own answers.

We were the ones asking for a chance.

At 19, Dinos and Takis wanted to move to San Francisco. They had no network, little money, and a lot to figure out.

Three years of mistakes, unanswered emails, and a few people saying yes brought the team to Silicon Valley. Philoxenia is how we pass that access on.

philoxenia/filo·kse·ˈni·a/

From the Greek philos (friend) + xenos (stranger). The wish to open your home, your network, and your time to someone starting out.

A few people who took the leap.

23 Europeans have made the trip across five batches. Some found startup internships. Others are raising for companies of their own.

A place for people who keep building.

You don’t need to have it all figured out. Here’s who we’re looking for.

  • You’re 15–25 and based in Europe.

    Your starting point matters less than what you want to build.

  • You already make things.

    Code, projects, experiments. You try, fail, and try again.

  • Your ambition feels out of place.

    You want to meet people who understand why you keep going.

  • You care about solving a problem.

    You’re here for the work, the people, and what you can learn.

Before you pack your bags.

A few things to know about Philoxenia.

See application details

Who is Philoxenia for?

Builders aged 15–25, based anywhere in Europe. You should be working on projects and curious about building a company.

How long is the program?

Two weeks in San Francisco and Silicon Valley, spending time with founders, teams and other young builders.

Who is behind the program?

Dinos and Takis, the team behind Hustle Hours. Philoxenia grew out of their own move from Europe to Silicon Valley.

Where can I find the current application details?

Use the application page for current availability, dates and the application process.
